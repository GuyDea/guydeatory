---
title: What is an electron?
term: electron
slug: electron
summary: An electron is a tiny particle that moves around the middle of every atom. When lots of electrons move along a wire, electricity flows.
keywords: [electrons, particle]
---

Every [[atom]] has tiny particles called electrons around its middle. They are so light that
it would take about two thousand of them to weigh as much as the lightest atom's middle.
