---
title: 'How efficient is a heat pump?'
term: 'coefficient of performance (COP)'
slug: coefficient-of-performance
summary: 'A heat pump’s efficiency is described by its COP: how many units of heat it delivers for each unit of electricity. A COP of 3 means 3 kilowatt-hours of heat from 1 kilowatt-hour of electricity.'
---

A heat pump uses electricity to move heat, not to make it, so it can deliver more heat than the electricity it uses. The colder it is outside, the harder it has to work.
