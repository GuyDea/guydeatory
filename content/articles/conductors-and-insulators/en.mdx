---
title: 'What are conductors and insulators?'
term: 'conductors and insulators'
slug: conductors-and-insulators
summary: 'Conductors are materials that let electricity flow easily, like copper and other metals. Insulators, like plastic, rubber and glass, hardly let it through at all — that is why wires are wrapped in plastic.'
---

Electricity flows easily through some materials and hardly at all through others. Metals are good conductors; plastic and rubber are good insulators that keep electricity where it belongs.
