---
title: 'How do air conditioners and heat pumps work?'
term: 'heat pump'
slug: heat-pump
summary: 'Air conditioners and heat pumps don’t make heat or cold. They move heat from one place to another using a special liquid that soaks up heat when it evaporates and gives it back when it condenses.'
---

A heat pump carries heat, the way a sponge carries water. In summer it carries heat out of your house; in winter it gathers heat from the cold air outside and brings it in.
