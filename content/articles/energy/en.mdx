---
title: 'What is energy?'
term: 'energy'
slug: energy
summary: 'Energy is what makes things happen: it lets things move, heat up, light up or grow. It comes in many forms, like movement, heat, light and electricity, and it can change from one form to another.'
---

Everything that happens needs energy — a ball rolling, a lamp shining, you running. Energy never gets used up for good; it just changes form.
