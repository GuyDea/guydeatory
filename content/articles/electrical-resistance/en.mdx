---
title: 'What is electrical resistance?'
term: 'resistance'
slug: electrical-resistance
summary: 'Resistance is how much a material or a device holds back the flow of electricity. Thin, long wires resist more than thick, short ones — and the struggle turns some electrical energy into heat.'
---

Some paths are easy for electricity and some are hard. How hard a path is we call resistance, and it is measured in ohms.
