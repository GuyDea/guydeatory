title: 'What is temperature?'
term: 'temperature'

summary: 'Temperature tells how hot or cold something is. Everything is made of tiny particles that are always jiggling, and the faster they jiggle, the higher the temperature.'
---

We measure temperature with a thermometer, usually in degrees Celsius (°C). Water freezes at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C.
