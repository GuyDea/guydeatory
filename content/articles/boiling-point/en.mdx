---
title: 'Why does water boil at different temperatures?'
term: 'boiling point'
slug: boiling-point
summary: 'The boiling point is the temperature at which a liquid bubbles into gas. It depends on pressure: on a high mountain water boils below 100 °C, and in a pressure cooker above it.'
---

At sea level water boils at 100 °C. Lower the pressure and it boils at a lower temperature; raise the pressure and it needs to get hotter first.
