---
title: 'What is alternating current?'
term: 'alternating current'
slug: alternating-current
summary: 'Alternating current (AC) keeps changing direction: in European homes the electrons wiggle back and forth 50 times every second. Batteries give direct current (DC), which flows one way only.'
---

Electricity from a battery flows in one direction. Electricity from a wall socket keeps switching direction, back and forth, many times a second.
