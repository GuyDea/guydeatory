---
title: 'What is Ohm’s law?'
term: 'Ohm’s law'
slug: ohms-law
summary: 'Ohm’s law links voltage, current and resistance: current equals voltage divided by resistance. Push harder and more current flows; make the path harder and less flows.'
---

Ohm’s law is a simple rule that tells you how much current will flow if you know the push (voltage) and how hard the path is (resistance).
