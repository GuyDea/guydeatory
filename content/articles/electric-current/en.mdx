---
title: 'How does electric current work?'
term: 'electric current'
slug: electric-current
summary: 'Electric current is a flow of tiny particles called electrons through a wire. A battery or a socket pushes them around a closed loop, and that flow makes lamps shine and motors spin.'
---

Electric current is electricity on the move: countless tiny particles drifting through a wire, all pushed along together. It only flows when there is a complete loop to travel around.
