---
title: 'How do we calculate electric current?'
term: 'calculating electric current'
slug: calculating-electric-current
summary: 'We measure current by counting how much electric charge flows past each second, in amperes. With Ohm’s law you can work it out from the voltage and the resistance: current = voltage ÷ resistance.'
---

Current tells you how much electricity flows past a point every second. Its unit is the ampere. If you know how hard the electricity is pushed and how much the wire resists, you can calculate the current.
