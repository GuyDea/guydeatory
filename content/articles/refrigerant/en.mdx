---
title: 'What is a refrigerant?'
term: 'refrigerant'
slug: refrigerant
summary: 'A refrigerant is the special fluid inside fridges, air conditioners and heat pumps. It boils at very low temperatures, so it can soak up heat even from cold air and carry it somewhere else.'
---

A refrigerant travels round and round inside closed pipes. It evaporates to pick up heat in one place and condenses to drop that heat off in another.
