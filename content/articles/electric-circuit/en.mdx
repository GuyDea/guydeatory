---
title: 'What is an electric circuit?'
term: 'electric circuit'
slug: electric-circuit
summary: 'An electric circuit is a closed loop that electricity can flow around: from a battery, through wires and a lamp, and back to the battery. Break the loop anywhere and the flow stops.'
---

A circuit is a complete path for electricity, like a race track with no gaps. A switch is simply a way to open or close a gap in the loop.
