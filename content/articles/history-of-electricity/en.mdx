term: 'history of electricity'

summary: 'Nobody discovered electricity all at once. Over more than two thousand years, curious people — from the ancient Greeks rubbing amber to Volta, Faraday and Tesla — each added a piece of the puzzle.'

People noticed static sparks thousands of years ago, but it took centuries of experiments to understand electricity and to put it to work in batteries, motors and light bulbs.
