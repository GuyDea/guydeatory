---
title: 'What is pressure?'
term: 'pressure'
slug: pressure
summary: 'Pressure is how hard something pushes on each bit of a surface. Air, water and gases push on everything around them, and squeezing a gas into a smaller space raises its pressure.'
---

When you pump up a bicycle tyre, you squeeze more and more air into it. The air pushes harder and harder on the inside of the tyre: its pressure goes up.
