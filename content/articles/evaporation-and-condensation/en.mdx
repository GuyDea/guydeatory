---
title: 'What are evaporation and condensation?'
term: 'evaporation and condensation'
slug: evaporation-and-condensation
summary: 'Evaporation is when a liquid turns into a gas; it soaks up heat, which is why wet skin feels cold. Condensation is the opposite: a gas turns back into a liquid and gives that heat back.'
---

Puddles dry up because water evaporates into the air. Drops form on a cold glass because water vapour from the air condenses on it.
