---
title: 'What is electric charge?'
term: 'electric charge'
slug: electric-charge
summary: 'Electric charge is a property of tiny particles that makes them pull on or push away from each other. Electrons carry negative charge, protons carry positive charge, and opposites attract.'
---

Some tiny particles carry something called electric charge. There are two kinds, plus and minus. Two opposite charges pull together, and two alike push apart.
