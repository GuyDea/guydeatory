---
title: What is an atom?
term: atom
slug: atom
summary: An atom is the tiniest piece of a material that is still that material. Everything around you — air, water, even you — is built from atoms.
keywords: [atoms, nucleus]
---

Imagine cutting a piece of gold in half, and then in half again, and again. At some point you
reach a piece so small that cutting it would stop it being gold. That smallest piece is an **atom**.

Atoms have a heavy middle and tiny, light [[electron|electrons]] moving around it.
