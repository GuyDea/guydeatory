---
title: 'Why don’t birds on power lines get shocked?'
term: 'birds on power lines'
slug: why-birds-on-wires-dont-get-shocked
summary: 'A bird sits with both feet on the same wire, so there is no difference in voltage between its feet and no reason for electricity to flow through its body. Touching two wires, or a wire and the ground, would be very different.'
---

Power lines carry thousands of volts, yet birds perch on them all day. The secret is that electricity only flows through something when there is a difference in push between two points.
