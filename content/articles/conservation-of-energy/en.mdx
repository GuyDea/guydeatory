---
title: 'Why does energy never disappear?'
term: 'conservation of energy'
slug: conservation-of-energy
summary: 'Energy can never be created from nothing or destroyed. It only moves from place to place or changes form — for example from electricity into light and heat.'
---

Scientists have checked it again and again: the total amount of energy always stays the same. When energy seems to vanish, it has really just changed into another form, often heat.
