## What is this?

Guydeatory is a library of explanations. Every question — *how does electric current work?*,
*how can a heat pump warm a house in winter?* — gets its own article. Each article starts from the
very beginning, so you never need to know anything before you start reading.

## Who is it for?

Kids from about eight years old who read on their own, and every grown-up who is curious too.
The main text of every article is written so that a child can follow it. Extra details for
grown-ups wait in “Go deeper” boxes that you can open whenever you like.

## How the articles work

- **The short answer comes first.** Then the article builds the idea up, one small step at a time.
- **Highlighted words are doors.** When an article uses an idea that needs its own explanation,
  that word is highlighted. Tap it to open the explanation. “Your path” at the top of the page
  keeps every step, so you can always walk back.
- **Try it yourself.** Many articles have diagrams and small experiments you can play with right on
  the page.

## Kinds of explanation

Every article is labelled with the kind of explanation it gives: **Big picture**, **Under the
hood**, **Numbers & formulas**, **Fun facts** and **History**. On the [Explore](/en/explore/) page
you can choose which kinds you want to see.

## Languages

Everything is written in English and in Slovak. More languages can join later.

## Privacy

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The site remembers only two small things, and only in your own
browser: the language you chose, and whether you like the pages light or dark.

## Is it correct?

The explanations are simplified, but they should never be wrong. When a comparison is not the whole
truth, the article says so — usually in a “Go deeper” box. The sources behind facts and numbers are
listed at the end of each article.
